# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in Agent Runtime Proof to be safe, constructive, and
welcoming regardless of experience, identity, background, or employer.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when discussing evidence or risk.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Make room for questions and different levels of security experience.
- Protect private data and disclose security concerns through the private channel in `SECURITY.md`.
- Correct factual mistakes transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing
credentials or private process data, and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or project-related communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates this policy, and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through
the repository owner's GitHub contact. Maintainers will minimize disclosure,
avoid conflicts of interest, and respond in proportion to the incident.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone publicly represents
the project.
